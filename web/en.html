What this is not

  • Not a forecast. It does not estimate the election result or the next government.
  • Not a single poll. It is a weighted average of polls published in the last 14 days.
  • Not demographically corrected. The site does not model age, sex, income or territory.
  • Not the full Congress. The hemicycle is a national D'Hondt simulation, not the real provincial allocation.
