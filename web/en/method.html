Method

A short English version of the Spanish methodology. The full method and source catalogue are at /metodologia.html.

What the average is

  • Published polls only. The observatory does not estimate voting intention from scratch. It aggregates polls that polling houses have already published.
  • 14-day window. The window counts calendar days including today. If fewer than four polls are available, it extends to the four most recent (maximum 90 days) and is marked as extended.
  • One poll per date and house. A house publishing more parties does not get more weight.
  • Weighting. Each poll weighs by recency (half-life 10 days) and, when the source declares it, by √(n/1000) from its sample size. Polls without a declared sample weigh by recency only.
  • Excluded. Partial or regional polls, averages of averages, and incomplete ranges.

Range, median and house contrast

  • Range is the minimum and maximum observed among the polls included. It is not a confidence interval.
  • Median is published next to the average. A large gap between them signals outliers.
  • IQR is the interquartile range: a more robust measure of dispersion than min–max.
  • House contrast uses the latest poll from each house in 45 days with equal weight per house. It is a robustness check, not the main average.

Source versus house

A source is where the poll was captured; a house is the pollster that produced it. One source can compile several houses. They are different axes and must not be added together.

Today: the top source is Otras fuentes (52.3% of weight); the top house is ElectoPanel (26.3%). Effective concentration: 2.73 sources and 5.74 houses.

A source above 50% is flagged even when no single house is above 50%. The site also publishes a leave-one-out sensitivity table: the average without the top source, the top house and CIS.

Seat simulation

The hemicycle applies national D'Hondt with a 3% threshold to the average of each poll date, using 350 seats and only statewide parties. It is a mechanical simulation: it is not a projection, not the real Congress and does not include ERC, Junts, EH Bildu or PNV.

Uncertainty and coverage

  • Sample declared: 78% of the polls in the current window declare a sample size.
  • No demographic correction: the observatory does not model age, sex, income or territory. Sources may use quotas, but those quotas are not published in a comparable way.
  • Method “no consta”: when a house does not publish its fieldwork method, the site says so instead of inferring it.
  • No attribution: this is not a poll-of-polls about who is “winning”; it is a descriptive average with explicit limits.
