Post-election calibration

How the pre-election average compared with the official count. This is a retrospective error check, not a victory claim.

Waiting for the 29N count. This page activates when official results and a frozen pre-election snapshot exist. No numbers are invented.

What will be published

  • Pre-election average vs official vote share by party, in percentage points.
  • MAE and mean bias: did the average over- or underestimate?
  • Simulated seats vs official seats, with the gap by party.
  • Snapshot date and number of polls; official source and update time.

Method

  • The pre-election snapshot is saved automatically in builds from 1 to 23 November; the last build before the blackout is used.
  • Official results are imported with scripts/import_resultados.py; no source, no publication.
  • Vote error uses votes for parties, not seats; the seat table is separate.
